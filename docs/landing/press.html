ONE PARAGRAPH58 words

Sveska is a studio-grade, local-first notebook that runs entirely in your browser. Notes live in IndexedDB, never on a server. There is no account; there is no key in the bundle. AI commands route through a thin edge proxy. The app is offline-first by default and works exactly the same when the network is gone.

articles · about pages · capsule reviews
LONG FORM142 words

Sveska — Bosnian for notebook — is an attempt to restore to digital writing the elementary dignity of paper. Built on the local-first principle from Ink & Switch, the app keeps every note on your device, in IndexedDB, tied to your browser and your domain. No account, no telemetry, no third-party scripts. Snapshots are named, restorable, and stored alongside the note as procedural memory. The optional AI layer — slash commands like /improve and /summarize — routes through an edge function whose only job is to be the place the API key lives, never the browser. Sveska ships M0–M3 in v0.1. The canvas, the AI proxy, the encrypted sync, and the platform layer follow. The studio is in Sarajevo and Brooklyn; the doctrine is in Bosnian.
