PRIVACY & SECURITY · sveska.studio/privacy · CLAUDE.md §5

Your notes stay
in your browser.

Sveska is local-first. The source of truth is your device. No account is required to use the product. The only network calls leave when you explicitly invoke an AI command — and even then, your key never lives in the client. This page is the threat model, the technical gates that enforce it, and the parts we don't gloss over.

✓
THE PROMISE

Zero personal data leaves your browser. Ever. Unless you click an export.

No analytics. No third-party scripts. No fingerprinting. AI calls only when you explicitly invoke /improve or similar, and even then the only thing sent is the prompt — never your other notes.

01 — Data

What we collect.

Two columns. The left is everything Sveska keeps. The right is everything other products take that we deliberately do not.

We do store, locally only

  • Your notes · in IndexedDB · tied to this browser + domain
  • Snapshots & versions · point-in-time copies you create
  • Preferences · theme, font size, slash-command list
  • Tabs & session · so we can restore where you left off
  • Inbox captures · until you process or dismiss them

We do not collect

  • Email / account · there is no account
  • IP address · the static site logs none we can read
  • Analytics & telemetry · no Plausible, no Mixpanel, no anything
  • Crash reports · errors stay in your console
  • AI training · prompts to /api/ai are not logged or used to train
  • Fingerprints · no canvas / WebGL / font enumeration

Honest about every actor. Green = blocked by design. Amber = mitigated. Red = the things you, the user, control.

04 — Hash-share & export

What you can choose to share.

You can share a note via URL (the body lives in the # fragment — never sent to any server). Plaintext, opt-in, with a warning. You can export .txt / .md / .html / .pdf — every export is a file you save, not an upload.

Hash-share is a feature, not a default. The CTA always reads "share via URL · plaintext" — no euphemism.
