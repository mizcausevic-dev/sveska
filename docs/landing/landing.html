01 — System

Three non-negotiables.

01Local-first

The notebook outlives the network.

Sveska is fully functional with zero network. Notes live in IndexedDB. There's no mandatory account; data does not leave your device unless you click an export.

STORAGEDexie · IndexedDB
02No keys in the browser

The security gate that fails the build.

AI calls go through a thin edge proxy. The API key only ever lives server-side, vaulted in env. CI greps the bundle on every push — finding a key fails the deploy.

PROXY/api/ai · edge · streamed
03Every project is a platform

The glossary ships from day one.

Sveska is a notebook and an SEO surface. Glossary entries auto-link inside notes and in exports. The marketing site, the changelog, the field notes — same component vocabulary, same tokens.

03 — Glossary

The words we use.

N. · BOSNIAN
sveska/ˈsvɛska/
A notebook. An exercise book. The unmarked surface a writer chooses before they write on it. Etymological cousin of vez — to bind, to fold.
see: notebook · fold · page
N. · COMPUTING
local-first
A class of software where the source of truth is the user's device. Networks are optional. The product still functions when the cloud does not.
see: CRDT · offline · IndexedDB
N. · SVESKA
snapshot
A named or automatic point-in-time copy of a note. Procedural memory — what the writer chooses to keep — not redo history.
see: version · restore · diff
N. · BOSNIAN
sevdah/ˈsɛʋdah/
A specifically Bosnian variety of melancholia. Untranslatable. The closest English approximation is "ache", but the connotation is durative — a settled, almost contented sadness that one inhabits.
see: saudade · Selimović
VERB · SVESKA
to fold
The action of marking a page as done — to fold it shut and move on. The amber accent is the fold; the pressed amber is the fold complete.
see: amber-700 · snapshot
N. · DESIGN SYSTEM
signal
The cyan color (#4FD6C4) reserved for live states — AI streaming, pending snapshots, incoming shares. Never decorative; only ever functional.
see: --signal-400 · AI
