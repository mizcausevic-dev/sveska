ABOUT · sveska.studio/about · the studio behind the notebook

The studio
behind the notebook.

A small team building one thing carefully. The premise: the page deserves the same dignity as paper. The rest is engineering.

Our doctrine.

01

Local-first, before convenience.

Every Sveska feature must work without a network. Sync, when it ships, is opt-in and end-to-end encrypted. The cloud is an option. It is never a tax.

"Software should outlive the company that made it."
02

One amber, no rainbow.

Sveska's design system has a single accent color and a single signal color. What is not added is the biggest feature. Restraint compounds across years.

"The page that doesn't yell at you is the page you read for hours."
03

Voice over volume.

We don't ship onboarding tours, exit popups, "next step" nags, or congratulatory toasts. The app is dry and competent. Bosnian first on display; English everywhere else.

"Stoic with a punchline."
04

Every project is a platform.

From day zero Sveska is also a glossary, a changelog, a field-note publication, an open-source brand kit. The notebook is the product; the studio is the platform around it.

"Ship the surface alongside the system."

Why Bosnian?

brand · in long form

The brand is in Bosnian. The product is in English. The reason is not exoticism.

The English word "notebook" is overloaded — by spiral-bound paper, by laptops, by a dozen apps. Sveska means precisely what we are building, with no rooms left over. The word arrives without the baggage of a hundred competitors.

The brand concept — Sarajevo Night — comes from the city, but also from the literary tradition we keep returning to. Selimović staged memory as procedure half a century before we tried to. Andrić wrote about bridges in a way that helped us think about pages. Karahasan kept a war journal of the soul. The brand is a debt we pay to that reading list.

The wordmark is outlined, the .studio is in JetBrains Mono, and the seam between them — the Bosnian noun meeting the modern surface — is on purpose.
