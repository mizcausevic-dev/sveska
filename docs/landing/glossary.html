N. · BOSNIAN · FEMININE

sveska /ˈsvɛska/

A notebook. An exercise book. The unmarked surface a writer chooses before they write on it. The plural is sveske; the diminutive sveščica. Etymological cousin of vez — to bind, to fold.
ETYMOLOGY

"From Proto-Slavic *съвѧзка — that which is bound together. The same root yields English weave, Latin vincire, and Sanskrit vāyati (to bind). Every notebook is, etymologically, a bundle held shut against the wind."

canvas N. · SVESKA · M5
The drawing surface bound to a note. Implemented behind a CanvasProvider interface — Excalidraw is the default; tldraw is a parked alternate. One canvas per noteId, lazy-loaded, exportable to .png / .svg / .json / .pdf.

caret N. · TYPOGRAPHY · COMPUTING
The blinking vertical line that marks where text will be inserted. Sveska's caret is 2px amber, blinks on a 1.1s steps(1) loop, and remains solid under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

A class of software where the source of truth is the user's device. Networks are optional; sync is opt-in and end-to-end encrypted. The product still functions when the cloud does not. Coined by Ink & Switch (2019); now Sveska's first non-negotiable.

sevdah /ˈsɛʋdah/ N. · BOSNIAN · UNTRANSLATABLE
A specifically Bosnian variety of melancholia. The closest English approximation is ache, but the connotation is durative — a settled, almost contented sadness that one inhabits. Comparable to Portuguese saudade but more sociable; sevdah is sung in groups, over rakija, well past midnight.
see: saudade · Selimović6 backlinks
snapshot N. · SVESKA · M1.2
A named or automatic point-in-time copy of a note. Procedural memory — what the writer chooses to keep — not redo history. The snapshot dot in the toolbar is the system's heartbeat.

The brand concept. Tradition (paper, ink, the folded page) meets innovation (the caret, the canvas). Dark-mode first. The palette is named after the city the team can't stop reading about.

To mark a page as done — to fold it shut and move on. The amber accent is the fold; the pressed amber (-700) is the fold complete. The mark's geometry is a folded corner.
