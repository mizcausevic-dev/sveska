The folded page
does not need permission.

312 words · 14 min read · snapshot 14:02 · 7 revisions · auto-linked 5 terms

The folded page does not need permission to be written on. Local-first means the app keeps working when the network does not. Sveska1 — the Bosnian word for notebook — is built on this premise: your notes outlive the network, and the cloud is an option, not a tax.

We are tired of "products" that demand an account before they will let you type. The redo stack is loss-less; procedural memory2 is what the writer chooses to keep. The app makes the second one easy, and the first one invisible.

What lives where

The note body, the snapshots, the canvas, the tabs — everything lives in IndexedDB3, in your browser, under your domain. No keys ship in the bundle. AI calls route through an edge function whose only job is to be the place the key isn't. 

// CLAUDE.md §5 — the security gate
$ grep -r -E "(sk-[a-z0-9-_]{20,}|VITE_.*_API_KEY)" dist/

That grep runs on every commit. A leak fails the build. The browser is not a vault; it never has to be.

Reading notes

Selimović4's Death and the Dervish stages memory as procedure. The protagonist's relationship to time isn't sequential; it's recursive. Every chapter folds back into the same handful of fixed points. Useful frame for a notebook app — the snapshot as a recursive anchor, not a linear save.

"There is no greater silence than the silence after a great noise. I sit in mine, and the city goes on without me."
— Meša Selimović, ch. 14

Andrić's bridges work the same way. Every conflict in The Bridge on the Drina is staged as a passage — an object that holds two banks in tension without resolving them. The page does this too.

What we're calling things

Sveska's vocabulary is small and intentional. Sevdah5 is borrowed from sevdalinka — the slow songs of Sarajevo, a durative kind of grief. The product borrows it back: a settled, contented relationship to making things slowly.

Three terms, one principle: seam (where an interface meets its impl), signal (the cyan that marks anything alive), fold (to mark a page as done, and move on).

System ledger

  1. Local-first. Source of truth is your device. Always.
  2. No keys in the browser. CI fails the build if a key is found.
  3. Every project is a platform. Glossary, blog, and SEO ship from day zero.
  4. Swappable canvas. Excalidraw v1; tldraw behind a flag.
  5. Performance budget. TTI < 1.5s, bundle < 180 KB gz.
