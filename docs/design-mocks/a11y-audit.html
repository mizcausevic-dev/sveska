02 — Focus

Where the keyboard lands.

A 2 px amber ring with a 2 px transparent inset — always visible, never blurred. Skip :focus (mouse-only), always use :focus-visible. Components that already carry amber (primary button) get a ring offset; ghost variants invert the border.

03 — Keyboard

Every action is a keystroke.

Sveska is keyboard-first. The audit rule of thumb — if you can't reach it without a mouse, the design isn't done. Tab order follows visual reading order, in DOM order, top-left to bottom-right. No tabindex > 0 anywhere.

04 — Screen reader

What we announce.

Sveska tested with VoiceOver (macOS, iOS) and NVDA (Windows). The amber accent and cyan signal are color-only cues — they must be paired with text or ARIA so screen-reader users get the same information.

05 — Color blindness

Amber and cyan, separated by hue.

Sveska's signal colors are intentionally far apart on the hue wheel — amber (yellow-orange) and cyan (blue-green) read as distinct under deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia. We also pair every color cue with a glyph or text so the audit doesn't depend on hue alone.

06 — Tap targets

Mobile · 44 minimum, 48 preferred.

From tokens.css · --tap: 44px. Apple HIG and WCAG 2.5.5 both call for 44; Material asks for 48. Sveska's mobile chrome (toolbar buttons, tab targets, FAB) sits at 44–48 px.

07 — Reduced motion

Honored at the source.

See the Motion Spec for full timing. Every motion atom in motion.css has a @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) override. Sveska also exposes a manual override at /settings/appearance · Reduced motion: auto · off · on.

08 — CI gates

Audit on every push.

The a11y posture above is not aspirational — it's enforced. Three gates run on every push; failing any one fails the build.
