15Tone palette · the chord of all 14SUMMARY

What we say, when nothing is happening — or something went wrong.

writing guide · BRAND §4 · paired with all states above
DO
  • "Prazna sveska. Najbolji početak."
  • "Caught up."
  • "The notebook is getting heavy."
  • "Couldn't save the snapshot. The note is still in memory."
  • "AI is a capability, not a coupling."
MAYBE
  • "Looks like there's nothing here yet."
  • "We hit a snag." — too casual without the fix
  • Anything with "Oops" or "Whoops" — never
NEVER
  • "Uh oh! Something went wrong 😬"
  • "Don't worry, your data is safe!"
  • "Try refreshing the page!"
  • "Oopsie daisy"
  • Any emoji in an error state
